01

WEB PLATFORM

HennyAdmin / Liquor Bee web dashboard

New features

01

Manage multiple stores from one login

  • Sign in once and switch between linked stores.
  • See sales across all locations, then open an individual store for more detail.
  • Compare store sales trends, cash and credit sales, taxes, category sales, and inventory value.
  • Review purchase orders across locations, including orders ready to receive, upcoming deliveries, and recently received orders.
  • Build a consolidated reorder cart with store-level quantities, suggested cases, sales history, and warehouse options for supported Alabama stores.
02

More complete wholesale ordering

  • Place orders for customers from the management dashboard, with account selection, catalog search, editable quantities, and line totals.
  • Review past orders and edit an existing order through the ordering screen.
  • Use Quick Reorder to select products from previous orders, adjust quantities, and add them to the cart at current catalog prices.
  • Switch between recent orders and the full available order history.
  • Save favorite orders from a cart or past order. Rename favorites, change quantities, remove products, and load them again for repeat purchases. Staff can also use a linked customer's saved orders when placing an order for them.
  • See stock on hand, quantities pending sale, and available quantities while ordering.
  • Manage linked customers and their house accounts, including account details and balance information.
03

Invoice scanning and receiving

  • Upload invoice images for automatic text extraction and item matching, then review the results before receiving.
  • Review scan batches and staged invoices, including products that need confirmation, correction, or linking to your catalog.
  • Use optional automatic staging to move processed scans into Purchase Orders. Items that need confirmation remain flagged for review.
  • Create purchase orders manually, search for existing products, and add missing invoice lines.
  • Receive by case or bottle, with units per case, costs, quantities, and suggested selling prices together.
  • Create new catalog items during receiving, including generating a barcode for products that do not have one.
  • Set up vendor templates and custom vendors to make repeated invoice entry easier.
  • See pending staging information and invoices ready for review from Purchase Orders.
04

Pricing and margin tools

  • Set desired markups by category and bottle size. More specific rules take priority over broader defaults.
  • Review current and suggested prices, cost changes, markup, and margin while receiving.
  • Configure rounding options for suggested invoice prices, including common shelf-price endings.
  • See missing markup-rule coverage so you can complete your pricing setup.
  • Use Price Check to scan or search a product and review its price, stock, cost, markup, margin, and vendor.
  • Use the Inventory Value Report to value stock at cost or selling price, view category totals, and optionally hide negative inventory.
05

Inventory counting and shelf review

  • Create named inventory sessions, choose categories, and record who is counting.
  • Scan or enter barcodes and SKUs to add or subtract counted quantities.
  • Review recent count entries and unfilled items, then move a session through review and submission.
  • Choose inventory export columns and download Excel or PDF reports.
  • Review slow-moving stock with sales history, days of supply, sell-through, and suggested actions.
  • Apply selected shelf-review markdowns or disable selected products, with a confirmation step and an option to print new labels.
06

Store routines and training

  • Use the 80/20 report to identify products contributing most to sales and review their stock and reorder needs.
  • Track four daily habits: reviewing key sellers, reordering, receiving, and checking dead stock.
  • See completion history, current and best streaks, monthly completion, and calendar and annual views.
  • Review estimated dollar impact with supporting activity details.
  • Access Liquor Bee Academy lessons, store assessments, working templates, a performance scoreboard, and completion certificates.
07

Invoices, labels, and app access

  • Customize customer invoices with your store name, logo, selected sections, item columns, and default notes.
  • Preview invoices and print open POS orders from the web dashboard.
  • Filter label selections by category, bottle size, recent shipments, or non-ABC items, and review the print queue.
  • Find Android app downloads in Account Settings, with descriptions explaining what each app does.

Enhancements

  • Expanded Alabama purchasing with a dedicated shopping basket, warehouse selection, and case/bottle ordering support.
  • Improved shopping-cart and basket layouts, quantity entry, and handling of larger product lists.
  • Improved filler-item organization and priority controls, including receiving with the configured TAP categories.
  • Added clearer product, search, and analytics tables to make purchasing information easier to review.
  • Refreshed the homepage, login, navigation, and store dashboards for phones and tablets.
  • Improved wholesale catalog loading, customer management, and order refresh behavior.
  • Expanded label printing and pricing support for Alabama and Louisiana workflows.
  • Improved invoice item matching, including closer-name matching and better use of known bottle sizes and case quantities.
  • Added a simpler LiquorBeeLite experience that shows the features available to the account.
  • Improved customer onboarding with clearer steps and representative management.
  • Moved nightly house-charge summaries to just after midnight Central Time so they cover the completed day, with total balances in the email and PDF.

Bug fixes

  • Corrected wholesale catalog pricing to use the customer's assigned price list where applicable.
  • Improved product identification in wholesale carts to avoid mixing up catalog entries.
  • Corrected order synchronization and editing behavior, including related history and refresh issues.
  • Fixed quantity-handling issues in carts and Quick Reorder.
  • Corrected missing current balances in house-charge summary emails and PDFs.
  • Improved invoice printing and template handling.
  • Fixed label-printing issues, including state-specific item selection.
  • Corrected case-item and case-break inventory synchronization issues.
  • Fixed product-name handling during synchronization.
  • Corrected DoorDash export filtering so inactive products are excluded.
  • Fixed homepage loading errors and improved dashboard performance.
  • Corrected enterprise sign-in/session behavior and registration-flow inconsistencies.

NEW ANDROID APP

New app: LiquorBee Mobile

An Android companion for daily store tasks. Latest source version reviewed: 1.5.

Launch features

  • Sign in to your Liquor Bee account and see today's sales and last week's top sellers.
  • Scan or search products with Price Check.
  • Select products for label printing.
  • Capture and review inventory counts from a mobile device.
  • Scan invoices when invoice scanning is enabled for your account.
  • Check for app updates and follow the download prompt when a newer version is available.

Enhancements and fixes

  • Added a visible “Up to date” or “Update available” status on the home screen, with tap-to-check access.
  • Fixed a blank-screen problem caused by pressing Back quickly or navigating back from a tool.
  • Corrected the service connection used by the app.
  • Corrected invoice-scanning visibility so eligible accounts see the tool consistently with the web dashboard.

03

NEW ANDROID APP

New app: LiquorBee Receiving

An Android workspace for scanning invoices and receiving deliveries. Latest source version reviewed: 2.13.

Launch features

  • Capture invoice pages, select the vendor, and submit them for processing.
  • Review and annotate invoice images before submission.
  • Review scanned invoices and staged line items, including unmatched and new products.
  • Create a purchase order manually or open an existing purchase order for receiving.
  • Search the catalog and add missing products to an order.
  • Enter case or bottle quantities, units per case, cost, and selling price.
  • Review suggested pricing and markup information while checking deliveries.
  • Manage the vendors shown in the scan picker, including adding vendors and hiding unused ones.
  • Check for app updates from the app.

Enhancements and fixes

  • Added missing-item entry directly to existing purchase orders, alongside staged-invoice item entry.
  • Prefill units per case from known catalog data and show the calculated unit cost when entering case costs.
  • Corrected markup percentages: a 50% markup now displays as 50%, rather than 0.5%.
  • Added clearer current and desired markup and margin information on staged invoice lines.
  • Highlight new products consistently without carrying the highlight onto unrelated rows while scrolling.
  • Improved scan-screen layout and button styling.
  • Kept support-only diagnostic email controls in Admin Mode.

04

NEW ANDROID APP

New app: LiquorBee Wholesale

An Android ordering and receipt-printing app for store terminals. Latest source version reviewed: 1.10.

Launch features

  • Place customer orders from a searchable catalog with stock, pricing, quantities, and totals.
  • Open directly into ordering, with General Customer / Retail selected by default.
  • Review orders and open an order's details.
  • Edit an existing order by loading it into the ordering screen.
  • Print receipts from order details or after order submission, using a supported USB thermal printer.
  • Use Open Orders Printing to print live POS orders.
  • See open-order, active-link, and open-request counts from the home screen.

Enhancements

  • Added a cart view with line removal and tap-to-edit quantities.
  • Highlight products already in the cart while browsing.
  • Added barcode Scan Mode for a compatible connected Zebra scanner; each matching scan adds one unit.
  • Added an on-screen number pad for quantity entry.
  • Show Pending Sale and Available quantities, with optional In Stock Only and quantity-limit controls. The quantity limit accounts for stock already pending sale.
  • Made Pending Sale easier to spot with bold blue text.
  • Added an explicit order refresh button and clearer order-created/order-updated confirmations.
  • Optimized the landscape layout and catalog search for large product catalogs on store terminals.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a crash when switching tabs before a screen finished loading.
  • Corrected the service connection that prevented live open orders from loading properly.
  • Fixed terminal filtering so orders without an assigned terminal remain visible.
  • Improved USB printer detection when a device does not report its name.
  • Replaced generic request failures with more readable error messages.
  • Corrected quantity entry that could accidentally append digits to a starting zero.
  • Improved order-confirmation animation loading and customer-facing order-number display.

NEW ANDROID APP

New app: LiquorBee Labels

A dedicated Android label-printing station. Latest published version identified in the release history: 1.7.

Launch features and enhancements

  • Sign in with your Liquor Bee account and use a dedicated station for queued label printing.
  • Use a visual style consistent with Receiving and Wholesale.
  • Check for app updates within the app.
  • Reprint labels from the print log for entries that have a saved label template.
  • Keep Current Queue up to date with automatic refreshes every 15 seconds while the queue is open.

Bug fixes

  • Improved USB permission prompts when selecting a printer.
  • Added explanations when the printer list is empty, including connection and host-port guidance.
  • Kept troubleshooting details visible instead of allowing another message to immediately replace them.
  • Added device and Android-version information to support troubleshooting.

NEW ANDROID APP

New app: LiquorBee POS Updater

A separate Android app that helps keep LiquorBee POS current. Latest documented version reviewed: 1.0.5.

Launch features

  • Compare the installed POS version with the available release.
  • Check manually or use a daily check, initially set to 10:30 AM Central Time.
  • Choose a different daily check time and see when the next check will run.
  • Enable the updater to open when an update is available, with guided setup for the required Android permissions.
  • Download the POS update for installation through Android.
  • Choose Later to dismiss that day's review while keeping tomorrow's check scheduled.

Enhancements and fixes

  • Replaced repeated hourly checking with a daily schedule that follows Central Time and daylight saving changes.
  • Corrected rescheduling so changing the check time can schedule another check today.
  • Preserve the last scheduled check result separately from manual checks, making missed reminders easier to diagnose.
  • Distinguish an attempted automatic opening from a confirmed on-screen opening.
  • Restore scheduling after a device restart and supported system changes.
  • Added first-launch permission guidance and a consistent download filename for future updates.

Automatic opening depends on the device's permissions and restrictions. Installing a POS update still requires the operator to complete Android's installation flow.
